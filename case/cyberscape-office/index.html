CYBERSCAPE

Customer
IT Company
Area
4 408 sq.m.
Location
Limassol, Cyprus
Status
In progress
SHARE:

ZIKZAK Architects, renowned for their innovative designs and attention to detail, seamlessly translated the client’s vision into the Cypriot office space. The incorporation of natural materials, neutral hues, and textured finishes reflects both the firm’s commitment to aesthetic excellence and their ability to capture the essence of the island’s stunning landscapes.

The first floor of the office building welcomes guests with a reception area designed in the style of holographic aesthetics, featuring a captivating palette of light purple, dark amber, light green, and light black. Nature-inspired compositions bring a playful and whimsical tone to the ambiance. The space is adorned with delicate materials, embodying the principles of oriental minimalism. Comfortable chairs are strategically placed for guests to relax, wait, and engage in seamless communication within this thoughtfully crafted and visually enchanting guest area.

Transitioning to the lobby, the office building’s entrance captivates with rainbow-colored LED lights, creating a vibrant and welcoming atmosphere. The overall ambiance is characterized by a sophisticated interplay of dark gray and violet, offering a harmonious and visually engaging experience to anyone entering this stylishly designed space.

The second floor of the office unfolds as a dynamic workspace, offering open areas adorned with universal modular furniture, versatile meeting rooms, and inviting recreation spaces. The palette includes vibrant hues of violet, to underline brand identity in small details.

Within the workspace, restrained working classics define the workstations, seamlessly merging with an abundance of greenery. This botanical infusion transforms the office environment, turning furniture into art objects. An office space embraces a vibrant aesthetic with bright chairs and a lamp. Notably, a lamp takes center stage, resembling an imposing Greek god, adding a touch of classical grandeur to the contemporary setting. The atmosphere is enlivened by glowing pastels, creating a dynamic and visually stimulating environment that fosters creativity and energy.

Moving further into the design narrative, the overarching theme continues with organic shapes and curved lines. The office space is elevated by illusionistic ceiling frescoes, adding depth. This stylistic choice infuses the environment with a sense of artistry and innovation, creating a workspace that transcends the ordinary.

The third floor exudes sophistication, embracing a refined and cosmopolitan ambiance. Modernity takes center stage with mirrored furniture and LED screen and lighting, creating a contemporary office space. The style, characterized by light magenta and dark amber hues, infuses a touch of glamour and elegance into the overall design. This modern office space on the third floor seamlessly combines style and functionality, offering a harmonious environment for both work and creativity.

The prevailing mood in the space is achieved through the artful combination of glossy black surfaces, luxurious marble, and sumptuous leather, punctuated by subtle accents of bronze and gold. The incorporation of natural stone, reminiscent of rugged rocks, adorns the hall, creating a mental journey to the serene sea coast and emanating a refreshing coolness. The interplay of materials and textures not only adds a touch of opulence but also evokes a sense of tranquility, making the environment both visually striking and emotionally resonant.

In sophisticated meetings room, meticulous design meets organic nature-inspired forms, creating an ambiance of understated elegance. The metallic surfaces and dark beige hues harmonize seamlessly with the meticulous detailing, showcasing a neo-concrete aesthetic infused with elements of oriental minimalism. The desk, paired with comfortable chairs, complements the overall theme, while a large picture adds an artistic touch, depicting detailed foliage that resonates with the surrounding natural elements.

Moving into a modern and classy conference room, the design takes on a contemporary allure with light bronze and black tones dominating the space. Utilizing vray technology, curved mirrors enhance the visual appeal, creating a play of reflections and textural layering. The eco-friendly craftsmanship is evident in subtle color gradations, and luxurious wall hangings add a touch of opulence, elevating the atmosphere to one of refined sophistication.

The inviting event hall offers a versatile setting for corporate events, internal meetings, or any prestigious gatherings. Moreover, meticulous attention to logistics and zoning in the multi-story office ensures that every employee finds their designated space, fostering a sense of belonging, while also allowing flexibility for individuals to seamlessly transition and work where convenience dictates.
