Home Office

Customer
Private client
Area
198 sq.m.
Location
Kyiv, Ukraine
Status
In progress
SHARE:

The brief was to create a modern office that keeps a connection with the architectural character of old Kyiv, without literally recreating the past, decorative imitations or details that only create an appearance of authenticity.

The story of the space begins at reception. Instead of a formal transit zone, we created a space with the intimate atmosphere of a cigar lounge. Deep wood tones, natural leather, stone, soft lighting and a restrained palette create a sense of privacy and calm.

Leather sofas and armchairs form a composition that feels more like a living room than a traditional office reception. It is a place for an informal meeting, a quiet wait before a conversation or simply a moment to pause and slow down a little.

At the same time, the interior remains distinctly modern. The clean shapes of the furniture and lighting contrast with tactile materials and the classical proportions of the space. This creates the intended balance: the historical character can be felt, but it never turns into decoration.

The large meeting table becomes the compositional center of the room and supports different scenarios, from formal negotiations to team discussions. The kitchen and bar sit right behind it, so the 2 functional zones read as a single continuous space.

Modern elements are integrated with as much restraint as possible: instead of competing with the space, they emphasize its scale and proportions. That is why keeping the full ceiling height and the exposed structural beams became one of the key design decisions. The architecture of the building stays visible and continues to shape the interior.

In the private offices, the overall concept becomes more restrained and personal. The goal was to create an environment for focused work while keeping the integrity of the project and the atmosphere set in the shared spaces.

Natural wood, leather, textile surfaces and stone create a warm, tactile environment. Deep natural shades do not overload the interior; on the contrary, they give it a sense of solidity and durability. The exposed beams and preserved ceiling height support the scale of the rooms, while integrated lighting allows for more intimate working scenarios.

We added elements more often seen in residential interiors: lounge chairs, bookcases, local lighting and small areas for conversation. Thanks to them, the offices do not look too formal or detached. They remain professional while creating a sense of privacy and comfort.

In this project, the historical context was never a decorative device, just as modernity was never an end in itself.

The interior grew out of the character of the building itself, combining its scale and memory with the functionality of a modern workspace.
